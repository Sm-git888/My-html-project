Flamingo

The flamingo is a tall and elegant bird famous for its bright pink feathers. Flamingos live near lakes and wetlands and often stand on one leg. Their pink color comes from the food they eat, such as shrimp and algae.

Bear

The bear is a large and powerful mammal known for its strength and thick fur. Bears live in forests and mountains, and they are excellent climbers and swimmers. Despite their size, many bears are calm animals that spend much of their time searching for food.
